## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)
ZigDash turns your phone into a fast, private control panel for a Zigbee2MQTT
smart home. Point it at your own MQTT broker and build exactly the dashboards
you want — no cloud account, no middleman, no tracking.

CONNECT TO ANY BROKER
• Works with any MQTT broker (TCP or WebSocket).
• Optional username/password; passwords are stored in your device's secure
  keystore, never in plain text.
• Live connection status and automatic reconnect.

BUILD YOUR OWN DASHBOARDS
• Create multiple dashboards per broker and arrange panels in a responsive grid.
• 13 panel types: Toggle, Button, Slider (brightness/position), Cover (open/
  stop/close), Multi-State, Combo, Radio, LED indicator, Node status, Progress,
  Text input, Text log, and Schedule.
• Map any value out of a JSON payload with a simple JSON path, so ZigDash works
  with real Zigbee2MQTT messages out of the box.
• Per-panel topics, QoS, and retain control.

SCHEDULED AUTOMATION
• A Schedule panel publishes a daily open/close automation (e.g. for shutters)
  as retained config to your own broker, executed by your always-on hub (such as
  Node-RED) — so it keeps running even when your phone is off.

POLISHED & PRIVATE
• Material 3 design with light/dark themes and Android 12+ Material You colors.
• Full English and Hebrew localization with automatic right-to-left layout.
• Back up and restore your setup as JSON.
• No ads. No analytics. No account. Your data stays on your device and your
  broker.

ZigDash is ideal if you already run Zigbee2MQTT and want a clean, customizable
dashboard you fully control.
