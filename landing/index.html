What it does

Builds itself

Finds your broker or Home Assistant, reads your Zigbee2MQTT devices and makes a tile for each one: lights, shutters, plugs, switches, sensors, thermostats. Custom MQTT tiles for anything else.

Signs in to Home Assistant

Share or paste the address from the Home Assistant app and sign in. ZigDash talks to Zigbee2MQTT through it, at home or away.

History

Temperatures, power and doors over days, recorded on your hub by ZigDash Engine even when your phone is off. Today is free; 7 and 30 days are in Pro, with a 14-day free trial.

Automations on your hub

"When the door opens, turn on the hall light." Rules run inside Zigbee2MQTT, with your phone off and the internet down. Three per home are free.

Alerts

A leak, smoke, an open door or a low battery reaches every phone of the home, even when ZigDash is closed, encrypted end to end from your hub.

Wall tablet

A wall display with a clock and a kiosk lock, free. Pro adds readings on the clock, wake on motion, a night schedule and alarms. Set one up

Without opening the app

Home-screen widgets, Quick Settings tiles and Android's device controls.

Hub backup

A daily copy of Zigbee2MQTT's settings, Zigbee network and device names, kept on your phone, to bring a reset hub back.

Remote control

From anywhere through the encrypted ZigDash relay, in Pro: one payment, no monthly fee, no port forwarding or VPN.

Private

Your devices stay on your network, with no cloud account. Anonymous usage data, which you can turn off.

Setting up

  1. Connect your phone to your home Wi-Fi. The scan only finds what is on the same network.
  2. Open ZigDash and tap Find my setup. It looks for Home Assistant, your MQTT broker and Zigbee2MQTT.
  3. On Home Assistant, choose Sign in with Home Assistant. Otherwise use your broker's login if it asks for one.
  4. Not found? Type your hub's address (for example 192.168.1.20) and tap Check this address.
