The MQTT dashboard built for Zigbee2MQTT

Your Zigbee devices, zero setup. ZigDash discovers devices from your Z2M bridge automatically — no manual topic wiring. No cloud, no ads, no account. Free and open source.

Get it on Google Play APK / Obtainium F-Droid — coming soon

Built for your Zigbee2MQTT home

Auto-discovery

Scan your Zigbee2MQTT bridge and create a panel for every device with one tap. JSON-path extraction understands real Z2M messages out of the box.

16 panel types

Toggle, slider, cover with presets, multi-state, combo, radio, scenes, text log, and more — lights, shutters, switches and sensors covered.

Any broker

TCP, SSL/TLS, WebSocket, WSS. LAN broker auto-scan, hardware-backed credential storage, automatic reconnect, instant multi-broker switching.

Remote access

Tailscale support — reach your broker securely over your WireGuard mesh. No port forwarding, no cloud relay.

Material You

Material 3 with dynamic color on Android 12+, phone and tablet in both orientations — ideal as a wall-mounted dashboard.

English + Hebrew

Full localization with automatic RTL layout (עברית).

Runs even when your phone is off

Schedules and auto-close rules are saved as retained MQTT config and executed by Node-RED on your always-on hub — SMLIGHT, Raspberry Pi, or any MQTT-aware device. Bundled flows included.
