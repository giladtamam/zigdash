Connect ZigDash to Home Assistant

Your phone can't find Home Assistant, or its address is hard to type? Make a code here on a computer and scan it with your Android phone's camera. ZigDash opens at its Home Assistant sign-in with the address filled in.

  1. In another tab, open Home Assistant the way you usually do.
  2. Copy the address from that tab's address bar, for example http://192.168.1.20:8123.
  3. Paste it below and scan the code with your phone.
