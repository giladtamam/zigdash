## Post 1: Zigbee2MQTT GitHub Discussions, a new "Show and tell" post

Link: https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/discussions/new?category=show-and-tell. There is no earlier ZigDash discussion there (checked 2026-09-27).

**Title:** ZigDash 2.0 look: feedback wanted before I build it

**Body** (paste as is; the images load from the public repo at a fixed commit):

```markdown
I make ZigDash, a free, open-source Android dashboard for Zigbee2MQTT over MQTT. There's no cloud, no account and no telemetry. Version 1.13, now on its way to the Play Store, has a new structure: device tiles, a Devices tab with health (low batteries, devices that stop answering), and a tablet layout.

2.0 is the visual redesign, and I'd like reactions before I finish building it. These four images are design mockups, not screenshots: the phone dashboard, the Devices tab, a wall tablet in dark, and the Hebrew (right-to-left) layout. The idea in one line: a warm, neutral background where amber means "on" and everything else stays quiet.

![Phone dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/giladtamam/zigdash/664c2f1550d8eb32d6e16f0e3204b221cfa11d23/docs/growth/2.0-round/1-phone-dashboard.png)
![Devices tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/giladtamam/zigdash/664c2f1550d8eb32d6e16f0e3204b221cfa11d23/docs/growth/2.0-round/2-devices.png)
![Wall tablet, dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/giladtamam/zigdash/664c2f1550d8eb32d6e16f0e3204b221cfa11d23/docs/growth/2.0-round/3-wall-tablet-dark.png)
![Hebrew dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/giladtamam/zigdash/664c2f1550d8eb32d6e16f0e3204b221cfa11d23/docs/growth/2.0-round/4-hebrew-dashboard.png)

Three questions:

1. What would stop you from using this as your daily dashboard?
2. Which of your devices has no sensible tile here?
3. Tablet on the wall: light or dark?

Play Store: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.giladtamam.zigdash
Source: https://github.com/giladtamam/zigdash
```
