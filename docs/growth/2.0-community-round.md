## Post 1: Zigbee2MQTT GitHub Discussions (category: Show and tell)

**Title:** ZigDash 2.0 look: feedback wanted before I build it

I make ZigDash, a free Android dashboard for Zigbee2MQTT over MQTT (no cloud, no account, no telemetry). Version 1.13 just shipped a new structure: device tiles, a Devices tab with health (low batteries, silent devices), and a tablet layout. 2.0 is the visual redesign, and I'd like reactions before writing any of it.

The four images show the phone dashboard, the Devices tab, a wall tablet in dark, and the Hebrew (right-to-left) layout. Idea in one line: warm neutral background, amber means "on", everything else stays quiet.

Three questions:

1. What would stop you from using this as your daily dashboard?
2. Which of your devices has no sensible tile here?
3. Tablet on the wall: light or dark?

Play Store: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.giladtamam.zigdash
