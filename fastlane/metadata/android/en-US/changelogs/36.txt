ZigDash 2.3: alerts. Get a notification for a leak, smoke, a door opened at night or a low battery, even with ZigDash closed. Node-RED on your hub does the watching; ZigDash sets it up in one tap, and the notification reaches your phone through Google's push, encrypted end to end. No account, no extra app. Share a Home's alerts with the household, or get them with the ntfy app on a phone without Google services.
