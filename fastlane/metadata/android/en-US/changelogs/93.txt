New: Hub backup. Once a day, ZigDash keeps a copy of Zigbee2MQTT's settings, Zigbee network and device names from your hub, encrypted on your phone. If a hub is reset or wiped, restore the network from Settings, Home, Hub backup, instead of pairing every device again. You can also save a copy as Zigbee2MQTT's own backup zip. Import from Home Assistant now checks the address first.
