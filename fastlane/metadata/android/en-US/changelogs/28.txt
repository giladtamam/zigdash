A redesigned ZigDash. Devices are tiles: tap the icon to switch, the tile for brightness, colour or position. Add tile lists your Zigbee devices first; one Edit mode arranges everything, with Undo. Offline tiles keep their last values. The Devices tab shows what needs attention (low batteries, silent devices), and each device has its own page. Settings lists your homes. Tablets get a side rail and more columns. Your tiles and homes stay as they are.
