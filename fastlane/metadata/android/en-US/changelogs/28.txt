Devices got a real home. The Devices tab puts what needs attention first: low batteries, devices that stop answering, failed pairings. Tap a device for its page: controls, readings, health and the dashboards it is on. Settings now lists your homes, each with its own page. On tablets and wide screens, a side rail, more columns and Devices beside its details. Custom MQTT tiles start from the topic, or pick a device. Your tiles and homes stay as they are.
