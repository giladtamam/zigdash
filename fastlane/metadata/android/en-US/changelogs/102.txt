Connecting through Home Assistant? You no longer have to type its address. In the Home Assistant app, open Settings › Companion app, tap your server, then the share icon at the top, and pick ZigDash. Or copy the link there and tap the new Paste button in ZigDash.

Usage data, for those who share it, now says how the address was given (typed, found, pasted or shared), never the address itself. The privacy policy lists it.
