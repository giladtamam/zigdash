ZigDash is a fast Zigbee2MQTT dashboard for your phone and a wall tablet. Sign in with Home Assistant if Zigbee2MQTT runs there, or connect to your MQTT broker, and your dashboard builds itself. No account, no ads.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TWO WAYS TO CONNECT
• Home Assistant: sign in, and ZigDash finds Zigbee2MQTT inside it. Share or paste the address from the Home Assistant app, no typing.
• Your MQTT broker: Mosquitto, EMQX, an SMLIGHT hub or a Raspberry Pi. The setup scan looks for it on your Wi-Fi.
• No hub yet? Look around the demo home.

THE DASHBOARD BUILDS ITSELF
• Every Zigbee2MQTT device gets a tile: lights, shutters, plugs, switches, sensors, thermostats.
• Rooms and scenes, plus custom MQTT tiles for anything else on your broker.

HISTORY
• Temperatures, power and doors over days, recorded on your hub by ZigDash Engine, even when your phone is off. Today is free; 7 and 30 days are in Pro, with a 14-day free trial.

AUTOMATIONS ON YOUR HUB
• "When the door opens, turn on the hall light." ZigDash Engine runs your rules inside Zigbee2MQTT, so they work with your phone off and the internet down. Three per home are free.

ALERTS
• A leak, smoke, an open door or a low battery: a notification on every phone of the home, even with ZigDash closed, encrypted end to end from your hub.

WALL TABLET
• A wall display with a clock and a kiosk lock, free. Pro adds readings on the clock, wake on motion, a night schedule and alarms.

WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
• Home-screen widgets, Quick Settings tiles and Android's device controls.

HUB BACKUP
• A daily copy of Zigbee2MQTT's settings, Zigbee network and device names, kept on your phone, to bring a reset hub back.

REMOTE CONTROL
• Control your home from anywhere through the encrypted ZigDash relay: one payment, no monthly fee, no port forwarding or VPN. Homes connected through Home Assistant work away from home wherever your Home Assistant address does.

HELP INSIDE THE APP
• An assistant answers questions about tiles, devices and automations in your language, and a person is one tap away.

PRIVATE
• Your devices stay on your network: no cloud account. Anonymous usage data, which you can turn off. Open source (MIT).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT YOU NEED
• Zigbee2MQTT, in Home Assistant, on an SMLIGHT hub, a Raspberry Pi or in Docker. Automations and History need Zigbee2MQTT 2.4 or newer.
• ZHA (Home Assistant's built-in Zigbee) is not supported.

ZigDash Pro is one payment: History over days, unlimited automations, the wall tablet extras and remote control. Everything that is free today stays free.
