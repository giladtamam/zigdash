ZigDash is a dashboard for Zigbee2MQTT. A small hub running Zigbee2MQTT and ZigDash on your iPhone or iPad are the whole setup: no Home Assistant, no server to maintain, no cloud. Already have Home Assistant? ZigDash works with it too.

ZigDash finds your hub on your home network and builds your dashboard from your devices, so you control lights, blinds, plugs and sensors a minute after installing. No account, no ads.

Needs Zigbee2MQTT and an MQTT broker on your network (an SMLIGHT hub, a Raspberry Pi or any Linux box). No Zigbee2MQTT yet? Try the demo home.

SET UP IN A MINUTE
• Find my setup scans your network for the broker and Zigbee2MQTT, and says in plain words what to fix when something fails.
• Pick your devices and get a dashboard grouped by room and kind.
• Using Home Assistant? Connect through it and import your device names, rooms and dashboards.

CONTROL EVERYTHING
• Lights with brightness, white and colour; blinds with open, stop, close and position; plugs with power readings; scenes; sensors at a glance.
• Every device has its own page with all its controls and today's history.
• Last known values stay on screen when the hub can't be reached, marked as stale.

AUTOMATIONS THAT RUN ON THE HUB
• Simple rules and schedules run on your hub, so they keep working when your phone is off or away.

THE WALL TABLET
• Turn an iPad into a wall display: fullscreen, dimming, a clock when idle and a PIN lock for kiosk use (with Guided Access).
• With Pro: readings on the clock, wake on motion, a night schedule, and alarms that fill the screen for leaks and smoke.

REMOTE CONTROL
• Reach your home from anywhere through the ZigDash relay, end-to-end encrypted between your hub and your phones. Or use your own VPN, such as Tailscale.

ZIGDASH PRO
Setup, dashboards, every device control, three automations per home and the wall tablet stay free. ZigDash Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, no subscription: unlimited automations, remote control through the relay, 7- and 30-day history, the wall tablet's extras, and every Pro feature that comes later. It keeps an app built by one person going.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your homes and devices stay on your phone and your broker. Passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain.
• Anonymous usage data helps fix setup problems; you can turn it off in one tap.
• Open source (MIT): the code is public.
